Easy to fly. Hard to master.

Drag to steer

Drag anywhere on the screen to fly up, down, left and right through the rings, past towers, beams, walls and sliding crates.

Flips and barrel rolls

Rolls dash you sideways out of trouble. Flip through a ring for double points, and chain stunts and rings for a combo of up to x5.

Watch your battery

It drains as you fly. Catch lightning bolts or spend coins to recharge, before your drone drops out of the sky.

Five worlds

From Sunset Meadow over Harbour Coast, Autumn Canyon and Winter Peaks into Night City.

Seven drones

Collect coins and paint your drone, from Seafoam and Plum to the shiny Gold Rush.

Leaderboards

Weekly and all-time boards for the best flights. Sign in with Google or Facebook, or fly as a guest.
