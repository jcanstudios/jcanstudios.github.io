Drone Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Drone Master is a mobile game made by JCAN Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email aerol.unay@gmail.com.

Your age decides how the game works

The first time the game opens, it asks for your birth year. Players under 13 get kids' mode (see "Children" below); everyone else can sign in with Google or Facebook, or fly as a guest. The birth year is kept only on your device; it is not sent to us.

Playing without signing in

You don't have to sign in to play. Players 13 and over can skip signing in: everything is then kept only on your device, and you don't appear on the leaderboards. Or tap Guest to fly under a made-up pilot name (such as "Zippy Rotor 42") with an anonymous account that has no name, email address or photo, only a random account ID. Ads are shown either way (see "Advertising" below).

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, profile photo, email address and an account ID from that service. We use them to create and identify your game account. Your email address is never shown in the game.

Guest and kids' accounts have only a random account ID.

Game data linked to your account (signed-in players, guests and kids):

  • Cloud save: your coins, best score and distance, number of flights, the drones you own and which one you fly, so they carry over to a new phone.
  • Leaderboards: your best flight this week and of all time (score, distance and how long it lasted), with the name and photo shown next to it (see below).

On your device only: your sound, music, vibration and control settings, and your birth year. If you don't sign in, your coins, scores and drones stay only on your device too.

The game has no friends, chat or messages, and no real-money purchases. Coins are virtual and can only be earned in the game.

What other players can see

The weekly and all-time leaderboards show each player's best score and distance, with:

  • for players signed in with Google or Facebook, their first name and last initial (for example "Aerol U.") and their profile photo;
  • for guests and kids, only their made-up pilot name, with no photo.

Anyone using the app can see the leaderboards.

How we use information

  • To run your account and keep your cloud save.
  • To run the leaderboards, and to keep out impossible scores.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner below the game, full-screen ads between flights (never during one), and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch at the end of a flight (to keep flying or to double your coins).

In kids' mode, ads are requested as child-directed: Google does not show personalised ads, only ads suitable for children, and limits the information it collects as its policies for children's apps require.

For players 13 and over, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, and information about the ads you see and tap, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent first. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sharing

When you tap Share at the end of a flight, the game makes a picture of your score and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you choose to. Sharing is turned off in kids' mode.

Services we use

We use these companies to run the game. They process information on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, cloud save, leaderboards, and App Check, which confirms requests come from the real app): Firebase privacy.
  • Google AdMob (ads): Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In and Facebook Login (if you choose them): Google, Meta. Facebook Login is used only to sign you in: the game doesn't send Meta any information about how you play.

Where information is kept, and for how long

Accounts, cloud saves and leaderboards are stored by Google Firebase on Google Cloud servers, which may be outside your country. We keep them while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and its data within 30 days (see how to delete your data).

Children

Drone Master is made for players of all ages, including children. Players under 13 play in kids' mode, which is designed not to collect personal information from children:

  • No sign-in with Google or Facebook. Instead the game creates an anonymous account automatically. It has no name, email address or photo, only a random account ID.
  • A made-up name. On the leaderboards, children appear only under a pilot name the game picks, such as "Zippy Rotor 42". They can't choose or type a name, and no photo is shown.
  • No social features. There are no friends, chat or messages, and sharing is turned off.
  • What we keep: the random account ID, the child's cloud save and their leaderboard scores. We use them only to run the game.
  • Child-directed ads only (see "Advertising").

Parents can ask us to see or delete their child's data at any time: in the game, open Settings, note the pilot name and Player ID, and email them to aerol.unay@gmail.com. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without signing in, or as a guest (players 13 and over).
  • Sign out at any time from the main screen.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing aerol.unay@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.
  • Remove the game's access to your Facebook account in your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.

Security

Accounts are handled by Firebase Authentication. Firebase security rules let each player change only their own save and leaderboard rows, and only with scores a real flight could reach. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add new online features. We'll change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the game.
