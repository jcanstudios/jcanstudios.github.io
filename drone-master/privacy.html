Drone Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Drone Master is a mobile game made by JCAN Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email aerol.unay@gmail.com.

Your age decides how the game works

The first time the game opens, it asks for your birth year. Players under 13 get kids' mode (see "Children" below); everyone else can sign in with Google or Facebook, or fly as a guest. The birth year is kept only on your device; it is not sent to us.

Playing without signing in

You don't have to sign in to play. Players 13 and over can skip signing in, or tap Guest to fly under a made-up pilot name (such as "Zippy Rotor 42") with an anonymous account that has no name, email address or photo, only a random account ID. Ads are still shown (see "Advertising" below).

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, profile photo, email address and an account ID from that service. We use them to create and identify your game account, and the game shows your name and photo on its main screen. Your email address is never shown in the game.

Guest and kids' accounts have only a random account ID.

On your device only: your coins, best score and distance, the drones you own and which one you fly, and your sound, music, vibration and control settings. These are not sent to us, and signing in does not copy them anywhere.

The game has no leaderboards, friends, chat or sharing, and no real-money purchases. Coins are virtual and can only be earned in the game.

How we use information

  • To run your account.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner below the game, full-screen ads between flights (never during one), and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch at the end of a flight (to keep flying or to double your coins).

In kids' mode, ads are requested as child-directed: Google does not show personalised ads, only ads suitable for children, and limits the information it collects as its policies for children's apps require.

For players 13 and over, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, and information about the ads you see and tap, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent first. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Services we use

We use these companies to run the game. They process information on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in accounts): Firebase privacy.
  • Google AdMob (ads): Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In and Facebook Login (if you choose them): Google, Meta. Facebook Login is used only to sign you in: the game doesn't send Meta any information about how you play.

Where information is kept, and for how long

Accounts are stored by Google Firebase on Google Cloud servers, which may be outside your country. We keep an account while it exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days (see how to delete your data).

Children

Drone Master is made for players of all ages, including children. Players under 13 play in kids' mode, which is designed not to collect personal information from children:

  • No sign-in with Google or Facebook. Instead the game creates an anonymous account automatically. It has no name, email address or photo, only a random account ID.
  • A made-up name. The game shows the child a pilot name it picks, such as "Zippy Rotor 42". Children can't choose or type a name.
  • No social features. There are no leaderboards, friends, chat or sharing.
  • Child-directed ads only (see "Advertising").

Parents can ask us to delete their child's account at any time: in the game, open Settings, note the pilot name and Player ID, and email them to aerol.unay@gmail.com. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without signing in, or as a guest (players 13 and over).
  • Sign out at any time from the main screen.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing aerol.unay@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.
  • Remove the game's access to your Facebook account in your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.

Security

Accounts are handled by Firebase Authentication. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add leaderboards or other online features. We'll change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the game.
