Delete your Stack the Cats data

Last updated: October 3, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Stack the Cats account and all the data linked to it at any time.

How to ask

  1. Email aerol.unay@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Stack the Cats account".
  2. Tell us the name shown in the game and how you sign in (Google or Facebook). If you can, send it from the email address of that Google or Facebook account, so we can confirm the account is yours.

We'll confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

Kids' mode (players under 13)

Children play with an anonymous account and no sign-in. To delete a child's data, open Settings in the game, note the player name (for example "Sleepy Kitten 42") and the Player ID, and email them to aerol.unay@gmail.com. We'll delete the account and its game data within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in link (name, photo, email and account ID).
  • Your treats, Tuna, Salmon, unlocked cats and wallpapers, and daily reward progress.
  • Your scores and leaderboard entries.
  • Your friends, friend requests, invite code and challenge history.
  • The records we keep of your games for fair play.

Deleting your account can't be undone: your treats, items and scores can't be restored afterwards.

Facebook

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove the game's access to your Facebook account: in Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find Stack the Cats and remove it. This stops the game receiving your Facebook information; to delete the data we already have, also email us as described above.

Data we don't hold

Ad information collected by Google AdMob is managed by Google. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings, and manage Google's use of your data at Google Data & Privacy.
