Stack the Cats Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Stack the Cats is a mobile game made by jCan Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email aerol.unay@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You can play as a guest without signing in. Guest games are not saved to our servers; only your settings (such as sound on/off) are kept on your device. Ads are still shown to guests (see "Advertising" below).

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, profile photo, email address and an account ID from that service. We use them to create and identify your game account. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Game data linked to your account:

  • Scores and best scores.
  • Your in-game balances (treats, Tuna and Salmon) and the in-game items you unlock (cats and wallpapers), plus daily reward progress. These are virtual items only; the game has no real-money purchases.
  • Your settings, such as the wallpaper you chose.

Social features:

  • Your invite code, who invited you, your friends list and friend requests.
  • Whether you are online or playing, which your friends can see.
  • Live challenge matches: your name, photo and score during the match, which your opponent sees.

Fair play: to stop cheating, the game sends details of each game to our servers (such as when each cat landed and how well), and checks that requests come from the genuine app on a real device (using Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity). Games that look automated may be flagged.

On your device: your best score, sound and music settings, and which tips you have already seen.

What other players can see

  • Leaderboards (weekly and all-time): your name, profile photo and best score. Anyone using the app can see them.
  • Friends: your name, photo, best score and whether you are online.
  • Challenge opponents: your name, photo and live score.

How we use information

  • To run your account and save your progress across devices.
  • To provide leaderboards, friends, invites and live challenges.
  • To give rewards and prevent cheating and abuse.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, and information about the ads you see and tap, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent first. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Services we use

We use these companies to run the game. They process information on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, servers, App Check): Firebase privacy.
  • Google AdMob (ads): Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In and Facebook Login (if you choose them): Google, Meta.

Where information is kept, and for how long

Your information is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be outside your country. We keep it while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and game data within 30 days (see how to delete your data).

Children

Stack the Cats is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Play as a guest without signing in.
  • Sign out at any time from the main menu.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing aerol.unay@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.
  • Remove the game's access to your Facebook account in your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.

Security

Your game data is protected by Firebase security rules and can only be changed through our servers. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the game.
