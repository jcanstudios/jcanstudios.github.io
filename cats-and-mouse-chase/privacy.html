Cats & Mouse Chase Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Cats & Mouse Chase is a mobile game made by JCAN Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email aerol.unay@gmail.com.

Your age decides how the game works

The first time the game opens, it asks for your birth year. Players under 13 get kids' mode (see "Children" below); everyone else gets the full game, including sign-in with Google or Facebook. The birth year is kept only on your device; it is not sent to us.

Playing without an account

You can play as a guest without signing in. Your coins, levels, unlocked mice and cats, items and settings are then kept only on your device. Ads are still shown to guests (see "Advertising" below).

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, profile photo, email address and an account ID from that service. We use them to create and identify your game account. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Game data linked to your account:

  • Your leaderboard points (this week and all time).
  • Coins won in live challenges. These are virtual coins only; the game has no real-money purchases.

Social features:

  • Your invite code, your friends list and friend requests.
  • Live challenge races: your name, photo and position in the race, which the other players in that race see while it lasts.

On your device: your coins, the levels you have opened, the mice, cats and items you have, how many rewarded ads you have watched towards each one, and your sound, music, vibration and control settings.

What other players can see

  • Leaderboards (weekly and all-time): your name, profile photo and points. Anyone using the app can see them.
  • Friends: your name, photo and points.
  • Challenge opponents: your name, photo and live position in the race.

How we use information

  • To run your account and keep your leaderboard points.
  • To provide leaderboards, friends, invites and live challenges.
  • To pay out challenge prizes and prevent cheating and abuse.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner, full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example to double your coins or unlock a mouse or cat).

In kids' mode, ads are requested as child-directed: Google does not show personalised ads, only ads suitable for children, and limits the information it collects as its policies for children's apps require.

For players 13 and over, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, and information about the ads you see and tap, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent first. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your result or rank and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you choose to. Sharing is turned off in kids' mode.

Services we use

We use these companies to run the game. They process information on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, servers): Firebase privacy.
  • Google AdMob (ads): Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In and Facebook Login (if you choose them): Google, Meta.

Where information is kept, and for how long

Your information is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be outside your country. We keep it while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and game data within 30 days (see how to delete your data).

Children

Cats & Mouse Chase is made for players of all ages, including children. Players under 13 play in kids' mode, which is designed not to collect personal information from children:

  • No sign-in with Google or Facebook. Instead the game creates an anonymous account automatically. It has no name, email address or photo, only a random account ID.
  • A made-up name. On leaderboards, children appear only under a name the game picks, such as "Sleepy Mouse 42". They can't choose or type a name, and no photos are shown.
  • No social features. Friends, friend requests, invites, sharing and live races with other people are turned off; children race practice players instead. There is no chat in the game.
  • Child-directed ads only (see "Advertising").
  • What we keep: the random account ID and the child's leaderboard points. We use them only to run the game.

Parents can ask us to see or delete their child's data at any time: in the game, open Settings, note the player name and Player ID, and email them to aerol.unay@gmail.com. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Play as a guest without signing in (players 13 and over).
  • Sign out at any time from the main menu.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing aerol.unay@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.
  • Remove the game's access to your Facebook account in your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.

Security

Your game data is protected by Firebase security rules, and points and prizes can only be changed through our servers. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the game.
